Featured Lesson: POP 3

6th Grade ELAR • Character Analysis & Coat of Arms

TEKS: 6.7(B), 6.6(E), 6.6(H) ELPS: 3E, 3H, 4F, 5F, 5G 60 Minutes

Lesson Objective & Sequence

Students defined heraldic symbolism and explained how authors use knights, armor, and crests to represent character identity in Freak the Mighty, followed by designing a personal four-quadrant Coat of Arms.

1. Hook: "Kevin's Arthur Clues" linking braces to armor.
2. Direct Instruction: Read-aloud Chapters 7–9; chivalric code.
3. Pop Culture Bridge: Anime/gaming insignia (Asta / Black Clover).
4. Application: 4-quadrant Coat of Arms & sentence frames.

Placement mentor: Mrs. Michelle Cerna • Jefferson Middle School

Classroom Management

Expectations, redirection sequence, and differentiation

Classroom Management Plan

"I don't talk over students. I wait for their attention, keep the routines the same every day, and give students the same respect I expect from them."

1. Focus on your own choices.
2. Report only serious concerns to an adult.
3. "Mute people with your mind." Tune out distractions and stay out of trouble.
4. Active speaker respect: Stay seated and quiet while instruction occurs.

Off-Task Redirection Sequence

Step 1 Name & Eye Contact
Step 2 Redirect & Move Seat
Step 3 Assign Detention

Targeted Differentiation

"I use sentence stems so every student has a way into writing and discussion, and I seat students on purpose so each one can focus and get support. Through Tier 2 dyslexia intervention and small-group instruction, I give students with IEPs and dyslexia the targeted support they need."

Sentence Stems & Frames: Multi-tiered written stems providing accessible entry points for Emergent Bilinguals and SPED learners.
Strategic Seating: Purposeful placement aligning peer helpers, proximity management, and low-distraction zones.
Small-Group Intervention: Targeted Tier 2 phonics, fluency, and comprehension reteaching during independent practice cycles.
Daily Entry Routine: 1. Binder & Pencil → 2. Seat → 3. Warm-up → 4. Lesson
