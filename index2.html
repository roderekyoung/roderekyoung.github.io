Why I Teach

Core Educational Philosophy • Section 1.1

"I teach because students deserve to see teachers who look like them. Growing up, I never had a teacher who was Black, a man, or a Black man. I want my students to have that. I believe connection is what makes students feel safe and willing to learn."

Connection is the foundation of cognitive engagement. When classroom structures honor student identities, maintain transparent routines, and establish high expectations paired with targeted support, every student can thrive.
